# Vendor email template

Replace the [placeholders]. Send one email per vendor, from the same template, so every vendor gets the same scenario and the same questions. Keep the list below in sync with `procurement-questions.md`.

---

**Subject:** RoPA tool evaluation: demo request and written questions

Hi [Name],

We're evaluating RoPA tools for [client] and would like [Vendor] on the shortlist. We'd like a demo built around one scenario, and written answers to the questions below.

**The scenario**

We are an EU company using a CRM, payroll system, recruitment platform, and AI customer-support assistant. We already have an Excel register, supplier contracts, and a retention policy. Show us how you import these, identify missing information, validate it with owners, and produce separate controller and processor records.

We'll send fictional data before the demo. We want to see the product itself with a business owner logging in, not slides. We'll ask for a couple of changes live. [Claude: suggestion — keep? Tells them changes are coming without saying which.]

What matters most to us is how a change in the business reaches the right owner and gets revalidated. Discovery gets us a first draft. Maintenance keeps it true.

**Written questions**

Please answer in writing. Verbal answers in the demo won't count for the evaluation.

*Hosting and location*
1. In which countries and regions is customer data stored? Can we choose EU/EEA only, and is that contractual?
2. Where are backups and logs stored?
3. Which sub-processors touch customer data? Names, locations and purposes.
4. Do you transfer data outside the EU/EEA? Under which mechanism?

*Support access*
5. Which countries can your staff and sub-processors access customer data from for support?
6. Is support access logged, time-limited and approved by us?
7. Can we restrict support to EU/EEA staff?

*AI features*
8. Which features use AI? Which models and providers?
9. Is customer data used to train or improve any model, yours or a provider's?
10. Can AI features be switched off per workspace?
11. Are AI-suggested values marked as suggestions with their source?

*Contract and safeguards*
12. Please share your DPA. Does it cover sub-processor change notice and audit rights?
13. Which security certifications do you hold? Please share current reports.
14. What is your incident notification time to customers?

*Language and usability*
15. Which languages are available for the interface, the questions business owners see, and exports? Is Swedish supported? English?
16. Can business owners use it without a full licence?

*Maintenance workflow*
17. Show how a change (new vendor, new purpose, changed retention) reaches the right owner and forces revalidation.
18. Are reminders and review cycles configurable?

*Cost and exit*
19. Price per module, per user, per record or per entity? What is not in the base price?
20. Implementation and onboarding costs? Who does the work?
21. Which export formats are available for records, evidence references and version history?
22. What happens to our data at contract end, and how long until deletion is confirmed in writing?

*Adviser use (if relevant)*
23. Separate client workspaces? Can an external DPO work across clients without seeing other clients' data?
24. Can the client control or revoke the adviser's access?

Could you confirm a demo slot in [timeframe] and send the written answers by [date]?

Thanks,
