# Lumen Outdoor AB: retention policy (excerpt)

Fictional. For demo use only. Version 2.1, approved 2023-05.

| Category | Retention | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Customer accounts and order history | 5 years | After last purchase |
| Marketing email addresses | Until the person unsubscribes | Unsubscribe or 24 months of no engagement |
| Payroll and employment records | 7 years | After year-end |
| Accounting documents | 7 years | After year-end |
| Website analytics data | 14 months | Collection date |

Not covered by this policy: job applicant data, customer support chat transcripts, partner-shop returns data.
