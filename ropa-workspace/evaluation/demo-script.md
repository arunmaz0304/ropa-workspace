# Demo script

Run the same script with every vendor. Score each step 0-5 and write what you saw, not what they said. Link each step to its criterion in `scorecard.csv`.

Scale: 0 not possible, 1 manual workaround, 2 partial, 3 works with friction, 4 works well, 5 works well and would survive daily use.

Opening line to the vendor:

> We are an EU company using a CRM, payroll system, recruitment platform, and AI customer-support assistant. We already have an Excel register, supplier contracts, and a retention policy. Show us how you import these, identify missing information, validate it with owners, and produce separate controller and processor records.

| # | Step | Ask the vendor to | Pass check | Criterion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Import | Import the Excel register and the supplier contracts live | Activities appear with fields filled from the files | Evidence and auditability |
| 2 | Evidence | Click any filled field | Source document or row is shown. Fields with no support stay empty rather than being guessed | Evidence and auditability |
| 3 | Gaps | Show missing information | Each gap is an assignable question or task with owner and date, not only a score | Business-owner usability |
| 4 | Owner validation | Log in as the HR lead and complete the recruitment activity | A non-lawyer finishes it without GDPR terminology; confirmation is recorded | Business-owner usability |
| 5 | Roles and output | Produce controller and processor records | Two different structures (Art 30(1) vs 30(2)); export is editable and includes sources | Register quality |
| 6 | Change | Add a new sub-processor and change a retention period | Affected records are flagged, the right owner gets a task, revalidation is required | Maintenance and change handling |
| 7 | Approval trail | Show the history of one record | Draft, confirmed, reviewed, approved are distinct, with who and when | Evidence and auditability |
| 8 | AI link | Add the AI support assistant | It reuses vendor, data and owner info from existing records | Wider privacy/AI links |
| 9 | Export and exit | Export everything | Records, evidence references and version history leave in a usable format | Total cost, hosting, exit |
| 10 | Your systems | Connect or map your real CRM and payroll | Connector works for your actual products, or the vendor says clearly it doesn't | Integration fit |

## Notes to take in every demo

- What did they skip or pre-load before the demo?
- What required a vendor consultant, not a customer user?
- What was shown as a slide instead of in the product?
- Which questions did they answer verbally that must also come in writing?
