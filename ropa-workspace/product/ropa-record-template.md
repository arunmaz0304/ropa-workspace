# RoPA record template

Two structures, one per role. The Article 30 fields are statutory. The Source, Status and Owner columns are product design: they make each value checkable.

Rules:
- A value without a source stays empty and gets a gap.
- Status is one of: `open`, `draft`, `owner-confirmed`, `dpo-reviewed`, `approved`.
- Check current Article 30 text and local guidance before relying on this template.

## Size exemption note

Article 30(5) relieves organisations under 250 employees from keeping a record unless the processing is likely to risk data subjects' rights and freedoms, is not occasional, or includes special categories or criminal-conviction data. Confirm applicability per client. Many SMEs keep a register anyway.
